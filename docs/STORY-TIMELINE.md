## ERA 1 — The war that broke the timeline (~1940) `[LOCKED]`

- WW2 happened, same shape as real history in outline, but **the bombs fell in 1940** — earlier
  than our timeline. The war ended differently as a result. Alternate history: same _feel_,
  different specifics.
- Nothing else about the war itself is specified — no theaters, no named battles, no reason the
  bombs came early. `[OPEN — NOTHING YET]` on all specifics of the war itself.

## ERA 2 — The long paranoia (1940s, post-war) `[LOCKED, thin]`

- In the years after, the American government grows **increasingly paranoid** — Soviet nuclear
  capability, internal dissent, its own people.
- "The crackdowns escalated. Distrust became law." — that's the _entire_ description of a
  presumably multi-year slide. `[OPEN — NOTHING YET]`: no specific incidents, no named policies,
  no timeline of _how_ the crackdowns escalated (a law? a purge? a surveillance program?). This
  whole stretch is one sentence of trend, not events.

## ERA 3 — Resonance, stage 1: small devices (**late 1940s**) `[LOCKED]`

- Government scientists, working in secret, find that nuclear blasts released something beyond
  radiation — a force that behaves like a fuel and a force, no name for it yet in-world.
- First devices: small, barely detect the signal. Tiny rifts open and close **in seconds**, on
  their own. **No creatures mentioned at this stage** — it reads as instrument noise. "Harmless,
  they concluded."
- `[OPEN — NOTHING YET]`: no program name, no agency name, no named scientists, no location for
  this first generation of devices (same site as the eventual city, or somewhere else entirely —
  unspecified).

## ERA 4 — Resonance, stage 2: larger arrays, first contact (**early 1950s**) `[LOCKED]`

- Bigger devices, bigger rifts, **longer durations**. Rifts still close on their own.
- **Creatures from the other side appear occasionally** — this is the _first point in the
  timeline anything crosses over at all_. Written off internally as anomalies, not policy.
- `[PROPOSED — this is the earliest point covert contact COULD have started]`: this is the
  earliest moment in the locked canon where a rift-thing and a government scientist could
  plausibly have stood in the same room. If the collusion (Era 6.5 below) has a start date, it
  can't be earlier than this — there was nothing to contact before now. **Not decided which
  specific encounter, if any, was the first contact** — flagging the _earliest possible slot_,
  not asserting it happened here.

## ERA 5 — Manufactured distrust (**early-to-mid 1950s**) `[LOCKED, with a new reconciliation below]`

- Existing canon: "The government can't wield magic or literal mind-control the way rift-things
  can — so instead it **manufactured distrust**, turning neighbors and factions against each
  other until the country tore itself apart." MK-ULTRA and Echo-SAGE flavor are the delivery
  mechanism, not just atmosphere.
- **`[PROPOSED — reconciling this with today's new idea]`.** The line above already implies the
  government _knows_ rift-things have a real mind-control-like capability it can't match — that
  knowledge has to come from _somewhere_, and Era 4 (first contact, early 1950s) is the only
  slot that exists for it. Today's idea slots in as the _missing middle step_:
  1. Having glimpsed real mind control is possible (rift-things can do it), the government
     tries to **build its own version** — Echo-powered mind-control devices.
  2. **It works on monsters. It does not work on humans.** (This deliberately echoes the
     _separate_ locked fact that raw Echo deforms/breaks nearly every human subject except the
     Dad and Son — two different failures of Echo-on-humans, same theme: **Echo and human minds
     don't mix.** Worth keeping as a deliberate rhyme, not a coincidence, if you lock this.)
  3. Unable to use the device on people, the government falls back to the low-tech version —
     propaganda and manufactured distrust (the _already-locked_ sentence above). **That's not a
     contradiction with today's idea — it's the fallback plan**, not a competing one.
  4. The failed-on-humans device doesn't go to waste. It gets handed to (or demonstrated for)
     the rift-power contact from Era 4 — see Era 6.5.
- `[OPEN — NOTHING YET]`: no name for the device program, no date more specific than "early-to-
  mid 1950s," no detail on how a government lab and a rift-thing established enough of a channel
  to trade tech at all (that channel is itself unexplained — first-contact logistics are blank).

## ERA 6 — Civil war ignites (**mid-1950s**) `[LOCKED]`

- `[OPEN — NOTHING YET]`: no named factions on the rebel side, no named leaders on either side,

## ERA 6.5 — The deal, and the monster army `[PROPOSED — new this session, direction only]`

This is today's new idea, placed at the only point in the timeline it can fit: **after** contact
is possible (Era 4+) and **during** the civil war (Era 6), since the whole premise is a losing
side reaching for outside help mid-war.

- The failed-on-humans Echo mind-control devices (Era 5) reach the rift-power collaborators
  the government is secretly in contact with (this _is_ the "elites secretly colluding" beat
  `STORY.md` already names, just with a mechanism now attached to it).
- **The monsters use the device on their OWN population** — not on humans (it doesn't work on
  humans, that's the whole point) — to muster a mind-controlled contingent of their own kind.
- That contingent gets introduced into the human civil war as a force multiplier for **the
  losing side** — your phrase exactly. **`[OPEN — NOTHING YET, needs a decision]`: which side is
  "the losing side"?** Two readings, both live:
  - **The government is losing** → fits the existing collusion line best, since it's already
    written as _"elements of the government"_ who are in contact, and the government already
    has custody of the Echo devices being traded. This is the path of least resistance if you
    want a fast lock.
  - **The rebels are losing** → darker, and flips the sympathy the "people pushed back" framing
    currently earns them, since it would mean the underdog side is the one importing a monster
    army. Possible, but it means someone on the rebel side would need their _own_ channel to
    rift-power collaborators, which nothing currently explains.
- **Reconciling with the existing "not a mass invasion" rule:** `STORY.md`'s collusion section
  already insists this "does NOT mean a mass organized invasion (the general monster population
  is still disoriented and un-organized)." Today's idea doesn't break that — it implies only a
  **small, deliberately-controlled contingent** was ever weaponized this way; the vast bulk of
  what pours out of the eventual open rift (Era 7+) is still the ordinary, disoriented
  population the surface story describes. `[OPEN]`: whether any _specific_ current or future
  boss/enemy in the roster is secretly one of this mind-controlled contingent (a good hook —
  nothing currently ties any bestiary entry to this) is undecided.
- **A thread worth pulling, flagged as MY inference, not decided canon:** `STORY.md` already has
  _"some monsters were sealed off — cut out of any ability to communicate with humans"_ as the
  origin of the demon companion (a defector who never wanted the war). Today's idea gives that
  sealing a possible _reason_ it didn't have before: maybe the sealed-off monsters are exactly
  the ones the collusion **couldn't** recruit — isolated by the collaborators to keep them from
  resisting or leaking the deal, or a faction that refused and got quarantined for it. This is
  not asserted anywhere — it's a connection between two existing pieces that would need you (and
  maybe Alisha) to actually decide it's true.
- **What this does NOT answer** (still fully open, per `STORY.md`'s own framing): _what the
  collusion is ultimately building toward._ Today's idea explains the **mechanism** (how the
  deal moved monsters into a human war) — it does not touch the **goal** ("IDK what yet" stays
  IDK).

## ERA 10 — The hidden layer (**NG+ / secret endings / repeat playthroughs**) `[LOCKED direction]`

Everything in Eras 5–6.5 above (engineered distrust, Echo-on-subjects, the collusion mechanism)
lives here narratively — it's **never told on a first run**. It surfaces only through repeat
playthroughs, secret rooms, and NG+.

- Echo-as-substance: the government doses test subjects with it; it deforms or breaks nearly
  everyone; leaders never take it themselves; the Dad and Son are the mystery exception. The
  **Echoes meta-currency the player grinds IS this substance** — already mechanically real, just
  needs the fictional label attached with intent.
- The demon companion: a defector, not a pet — first crack in "the war was ever simply monsters
  vs. us."
- Playing the other side: eventually, playing as / from a monster's perspective. Direction only.
- `[OPEN — NOTHING YET]`: the three-ending spec (Close It / The Truth / What We Are) is named
  only — no content, and it's blocked on an inkjs integration that doesn't exist yet.

## ERA 11 — The deepest layer: what are they building toward? `[OPEN — NOTHING YET, on purpose]`
